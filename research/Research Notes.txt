Hwaseong Campus Facility Profile

Confirmed independently across three sources (Korea Herald, BISinfotech, Korea JoongAng Daily), each reporting consistent figures:

- Investment: approximately 240 billion won (USD 163-182 million), 2021-2025
- Site: 16,000 square meters, two buildings (Building A: 11 above-ground / 4 underground floors, main offices; Building B: 5 above-ground / 4 underground floors, Repair and Reuse Center plus training center)
- Function: Repair and Reuse (R&R) Center handles repairs and part reuse for lithography machines used by Samsung Electronics and SK hynix
- Workforce: approximately 1,500 employees following transition, completed end of 2025
- Coordinates (ASML's own site, asml.com/company/about-asml/locations): 37.223639 N, 127.071167 E, 47 Dongtan-daero 9ga-gil, Hwaseong-si, Gyeonggi-do

ASML Disclosed Risk Factors (relevant to South Korea exposure)

From ASML's own annual report risk disclosures:

- South Korea represents 25.5% of ASML's 2025 net sales; operations and major customers based there leave ASML exposed to conflicts involving North Korea

This confirms the supply chain and customer disruption framing used in the memo's risk assessment: ASML's own disclosures treat South Korea exposure as customer and revenue-based, not facility-security based, consistent with this project's conclusion.
